# SpendWise

**SpendWise** is a clean, intuitive, and secure mobile app designed to help you track your daily spending with ease. Our goal is to provide a straightforward tool that gives you a clear picture of your finances so you can start making smarter spending decisions today.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- **Secure Authentication**: Create your personal account with a secure email and password login.
- **Email Verification**: We ensure your account's security with a simple email verification process.
- **Forgot Password**: Easily regain access to your account if you ever forget your password.
- **Add & Delete Transactions**: Quickly log new expenses and remove them if needed.
